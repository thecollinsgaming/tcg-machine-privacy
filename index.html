TCG Machine Privacy Policy

TCG Machine is a Windows app made by TCG Digital. It turns a creator's own game recordings into short vertical clips. Contact: hello@tcgmachine.app.

What stays on your PC

Finding highlights, cutting clips, rendering and the review screen all happen on your own computer. Your recordings and finished clips are not uploaded to TCG Machine.

What the AI service receives

Captions, titles and clip scoring use an AI service. This is the part that leaves your PC. Each request sends text only: the transcript of the moments the app flagged (with timings), the champion being played, the name of the session folder, and your licence key so the allowance can be counted. No video, audio or screenshots are sent. Requests go through a TCG Machine server and on to Claude, made by Anthropic. How long these requests are kept has not been published yet and depends on the AI provider's terms. It will be added here when it is known.

Connecting your accounts

If you connect YouTube or Facebook, the access tokens are stored on your own PC. They are used only to post or schedule content to your own accounts, and only for clips you have approved. The clip is then sent to that platform, and the platform's own privacy policy applies from that point. For platforms that are not connected, the app only opens the platform's page and sends nothing.

What is not collected

The app does not send crash reports or usage analytics. TCG Machine does not sell your data.

The website and beta list

The website privacy page at tcgmachine.app/privacy explains what the beta signup form collects and how to ask for your details to be deleted.
